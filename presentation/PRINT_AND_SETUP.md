# Poster printing and SEPLN setup

Requirements checked on 21 September 2026 against the [official SEPLN poster setup guide](https://sepln2026.org/guia-de-colocacion-de-posters/).

## Print file

Use `WomenHelp_poster_A0_en.pdf` for the English presentation version. `WomenHelp_poster_A0_es.pdf` is the Spanish review edition with the same figures and references.

- One A0 page, portrait: **841 × 1189 mm**, equivalent to **84.1 × 118.9 cm**. The forwarded email's “84.1 × 118.9 mm” is a unit typo; the official guide specifies centimetres.
- Print at actual size / 100%, on A0 paper. Disable “fit to page” or shrinking. Do not print on A4 and enlarge a raster screenshot.
- The PDF contains vector text, diagrams, charts and QR code, with embedded text fonts. References are clickable in the digital copy. The QR opens the public repository; evidence links use a fixed source revision so their content remains reproducible.
- No conference template is required. The present layout meets the specified page format.

## Attendance and installation

Bring the poster already printed. Conference registration is mandatory according to the organizer's forwarded instructions; preparing this PDF does not complete registration. Authors install their own posters in the assigned space. The organizers provide double-sided tape; do not puncture, paint or use permanent adhesive on the supports. Remove the poster at the end of the relevant day.

The guide places IberLEF posters on 22 September at Escuela de Ingenierías Fase II, Universidad de León, Campus de Vegazana, with supports available from 09:00. The [SEPLN program](https://sepln2026.org/programa/) currently lists the IberLEF poster session at 16:15–17:00. The setup guide still leaves the precise session time unspecified; confirm placement with the organizers on arrival.
